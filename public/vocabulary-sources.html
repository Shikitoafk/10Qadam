Where our words come from.

10Qadam combines open vocabulary profiles with English definitions. The imported collection contains 7,579 distinct headwords and expressions across A1–C1. Ten additional original 10Qadam entries are retained, making 7,589 study entries in total.

These are practice collections, not an official CEFR assessment or complete syllabus. A headword can have several meanings at different levels. The displayed band is its lowest supported band in the source profile; the English definition is not independently CEFR-graded. Some words have English definitions only. Existing Kazakh/Russian translations are retained; missing translations are not generated or presented as verified translations.

English definitions: WordNet 3.0

WordNet 3.0 Copyright 2006 by Princeton University. All rights reserved. Used under the WordNet 3.0 licence, including its full copyright and warranty disclaimer. Source and documentation: WordNet. It supplies definitions, not CEFR levels.

Definitions use the first indexed WordNet sense matching the source part of speech. Where 10Qadam already had a curated definition or translation, that existing content is preserved in the interface. A dictionary sense can be broader or different from the intended learning-profile sense; this is a study aid, not an endorsed learner's dictionary.

What we changed

  • Expanded slash-separated spelling variants into separate headwords; normalised whitespace and case.
  • Merged duplicate headwords at their lowest supported source level. A single headword is not counted repeatedly for different parts of speech.
  • Matched definitions by part of speech. Omitted entries with no matching definition, unsupported parts of speech, malformed headwords and C2 entries.
  • Retained the original study sentences, translations and word-game banks separately. Importing this catalogue does not change rated-match scoring or server word banks.
