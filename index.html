10QADAM — Spell Your Way, Step by Step.

Listen to English words, practise spelling and compete. A1 and A2 word banks, solo practice, one-on-one duels and four-player battles.

How to play

Hear an English word and spell it within 12 seconds. A correct answer earns 100 points, plus up to 50 for speed. Ten words per match. Most correct answers wins; speed points break ties.

English A1 and A2 spelling practice
