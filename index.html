Learn before you compete

Flip flashcards, hear words and remember them at your own pace. Listen to short sentences and write what you hear. Review character accuracy and see exactly which letters to improve. These are practice collections, not a certified CEFR assessment.

How to play

Hear an English word and spell it within 12 seconds. A correct answer earns 100 points, plus up to 50 for speed. Ten words per match. Most correct answers wins; speed points break ties.
